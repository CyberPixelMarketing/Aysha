Who Supports the Coach?

Coaches create thinking space for others. But where does the coach go to think about their own practice?

Coaching supervision creates a reflective professional space in which coaches examine their work, relationships, ethical questions, boundaries, challenges and development. It is not about right or wrong, it enables the coach to become more reflective, ethical, aware and professionally mature.

Designed for certified coaches, ICF practitioners and corporate internal coaches who want to elevate their professional practice.

Questions coaches bring to supervision

  • Am I approaching this client relationship appropriately?
  • Why has this particular case affected me?
  • Where are my professional boundaries?
  • What am I not seeing in this coaching relationship?
  • How can I keep developing beyond techniques and certifications?

Coaching As It Should Be

Frequently Asked Questions

What is professional coaching?

Professional coaching is a thinking partnership that enables you to develop awareness, explore possibilities, take responsibility and move toward meaningful action. It is not consulting, it is not mentoring, and it is not simply giving advice.

Coach, mentor or consultant, what is the difference?

A consultant brings expert answers. A mentor shares personal experience. A coach creates the structure and questions that help you reach your own decisions, and own the results. Much of Dr. Aysha's mission is correcting the confusion between these.

What is coaching supervision, and who is it for?

Supervision is a reflective space where coaches examine their practice, relationships, ethics and boundaries with a qualified supervisor. It serves new and experienced coaches, ICF practitioners and corporate internal coaches, individually or in group cohorts.

How is your excellence work different from consulting?

No report-and-leave. Dr. Aysha works alongside your internal team while solutions are built, so employees learn to operate, manage and improve the system independently, with periodic follow-up to assess sustainability.

Do you deliver technical AI training?

Dr. Aysha's positioning is leadership, coaching and organizational transformation enabled by AI, practical, responsible use for leaders, coaches, trainers and service teams. Specialist technical experts join when deeper technical depth is required.
